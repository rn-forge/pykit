# S22.1.2 — Cut settled briefs and repair references

Brief for

## Goal

Apply the approved dispositions in the inventory `_S22.1.1-inventory.md` (beside
the feature pages) to the 100 pre-brief spec pages: 42 trim, 21 move, 35 keep, 2
record. A trimmed or moved page keeps its identity, acceptance and unique
decisions and loses settled build detail, usage prose that a guide owns, and
obsolete procedure. No ID, filename, heading anchor that something links to,
state or closure changes. The inventory file is deleted at the end. The
deliverable is the edited pages plus a before/after word table and an itemized
disposition in `## Result`.
