# S22.8.2 — Own shared identity and record policy

Brief for
[S22.8.2](../F22.8-auth.md#s2282-own-shared-identity-and-record-policy), at
`7f11a7c`.

## Goal

Account-to-principal projection, the `SessionInfo`/`LoginTransaction` persisted
shapes and the fixed-window arithmetic are each written two or three times
across `rn-forge-auth`, `rn-forge-django` and `rn-forge-fastapi`. Give each one
owner in `rn-forge-auth`; adapters keep cookies, CSRF, backends and scheduling.

## Settled

- Settled by the reviewer, 2026-11-15: the `RateLimit` dataclasses in
  `rn_forge.django.auth.rate_limit` (`:18`) and
  `rn_forge.fastapi.auth.rate_limit` (`:24`) stay where they are; moving them
  would remove supported imports (ADR-0002 forbids an alias). Only functions
  over `timedelta`/`int` are shared.
- Settled by the reviewer, 2026-11-15: three new pure modules in
  `rn-forge-auth`, re-exported from `rn_forge.auth` (the facade must still
  import no adapter or extra; `tests/test_facade.py` guards it):
    - `rn_forge/auth/linking.py`:
      `link_bearer_principal(principal, *, accounts, contexts, scope_map) -> Principal`,
      `session_principal(info, context) -> Principal` and
      `class PrincipalLinkError(Exception)` (one `reason: str`; `str(exc)` is
      the reason).
    - `rn_forge/auth/records.py`: `session_to_record(info) -> dict[str, Any]`,
      `session_from_record(record) -> SessionInfo`,
      `transaction_to_record(tx) -> dict[str, Any]`,
      `transaction_from_record(record) -> LoginTransaction`. Records are
      JSON-safe dicts with exactly today's keys; JSON, caches and TTLs stay in
      adapters.
    - `rn_forge/auth/rate_window.py`:
      `fixed_window_seconds(window: timedelta) -> int`,
      `fixed_window_key(scope, subject, window_seconds, now) -> str` (digest of
      `scope\0subject`, `:`, bucket),
      `fixed_window_retry_after(window_seconds, now) -> int`.
- Settled by the reviewer, 2026-11-15: `link_bearer_principal` keeps today's
  order and messages: no issuer or no `accounts.lookup` hit raises
  `PrincipalLinkError("No account is linked to the token subject")`; an
  inactive context raises `PrincipalLinkError("The account is not active")`.
  Tenant is `context.attributes["tenant"]` only when it is a `str`, else
  `None`. `effective_permissions(..., mechanism="bearer", ...)` is unchanged.
- Settled by the reviewer, 2026-11-15: adapters translate `PrincipalLinkError`
  into their own exception with the same text
  (`AuthenticationFailed(str(exc))` in Django,
  `AuthenticationFailed(str(exc))` in FastAPI, still run in the thread pool);
  the framework exception types and the FastAPI failure log stay as they are.
- Settled by the reviewer, 2026-11-15: the persisted shapes do not change. All
  eleven session keys and eight transaction keys stay required on decode;
  `auth_time` is `None` or an ISO string; `amr` is written sorted and read as
  a `frozenset`; `extra` is returned as the stored mapping. No tolerance for
  missing keys is added.
- Settled by the reviewer, 2026-11-15: the existing public names in
  `rn_forge.fastapi.auth.rate_limit` (`window_seconds(rate)`,
  `window_key(scope, subject, rate, now)`) keep their signatures as thin
  wrappers over the new functions (different signature, so not a re-export).
  `RateLimit`, `RateLimiter`, `FixedWindowLimiter`, `InMemoryRateLimiter`,
  `RedisRateLimiter` and every counter (`cache.add`/`incr`, Redis pipeline,
  locked dict) stay in their adapters.
- Settled by the reviewer, 2026-11-15: secrets stay out of `repr`:
  `SessionInfo.id_token` and `LoginTransaction.state`, `.nonce` and
  `.code_verifier` become `field(..., repr=False)` (as
  `ExternalIdentity.id_token` already is). Equality and constructor signatures
  are unchanged.
- Settled by the reviewer, 2026-11-15: login-completion duplication
  (`django/auth/routes.py:335-375`, `fastapi/auth/routes.py:436-473`) is not
  touched here; S22.9.3 and S22.10.2 consume the new functions and decide it.
  No universal session or store abstraction is added.
