Django's request limits produce problem responses, not 500s, and an export costs
a constant number of queries whether or not the viewset shapes its queryset.

## S24.1.1 — Request field limits are a 400 problem

**State:** New

**Acceptance:**

- `GET :batchGet` with more `ids` than Django's `DATA_UPLOAD_MAX_NUMBER_FIELDS`
  (1,000 by default) answers a 400 `application/problem+json`, not a 500,
  whatever `transfer.max_rows` is.
- `problem_registry()` maps `TooManyFieldsSent` and `TooManyFilesSent` to
  `BAD_REQUEST`, with a detail saying which limit was exceeded and no request
  data.

## S24.1.2 — Export joins the resource's forward relations

**State:** New · **Depends on:** S24.1.1

**Acceptance:**

- A tabular export of a viewset whose queryset has no `select_related` costs the
  same number of queries for 3 rows as for 12 when the export resource reads a
  forward foreign-key field (`team__code`).
- `TestQueryCounts` pins this for an unshaped viewset beside the shaped one.
