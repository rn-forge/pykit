## Stories

### S8.1.1 — Record the split decision

**Status:** planned

**Acceptance:** Owner decides all four Django areas before the release tag cut and records the resulting package graph.

## Decision to record

Choose whether SAML, Celery, fixtures and messaging remain in `rn-forge-django` or move to separate packages. Record the chosen package graph and migration impact before [F9.6](../E9-release-readiness/F9.6-tag-cut.md). A later split would break imports, and compatibility re-exports are disallowed.

## Acceptance

- The owner records the disposition of all four areas.
- Release scope and manifests reflect the chosen boundary before tags are cut.

## Open questions

**Q4:** What is the split outcome? The timing requirement is settled; the outcome is not.
