# F8.2 — Review auth as one piece

**Status:** deferred · **Owner:** pykit · **Epic:** [E8](index.md)

**Entry criteria:** the owner schedules the review.

**Depends on:** [authentication across packages](../../../architecture/authentication.md) and [ADR-0006](../../../adr/ADR-0006.md).

**Source:** `plans/archive/plan-board.md`, "Backlog (revisit later, not scheduled)"; `plans/archive/auth-overview.md`, "My followup thoughts".

`rn_forge.django.auth` grew its login, session and token flows one at a time, and the web and FastAPI auth surfaces were added beside them. This review looks at all of them together, settles how a login hands over to the requests that follow it, and revisits whether SAML, Celery, fixtures and messaging belong in `rn-forge-django` ([F8.1](F8.1-django-split.md) kept them for release-1).

## Stories

### S8.2.1 — Review the complete auth surface

**Status:** deferred

**Acceptance:** The review records the login, session and token boundaries across `rn-forge-django`, `rn-forge-web` and `rn-forge-fastapi`, and whether any of SAML, Celery, fixtures and messaging leaves `rn-forge-django`. Any behavior change or package move it approves becomes its own feature before code changes.

## Owner note

Carried verbatim from the source:

> SAML is a login flow into the provider. once logged in, it is a post back into the backend from where it could issue a JWT and all furture frontend to backend conversations happen using that JWT

## Open questions

- Should a SAML post-back establish a session, issue a JWT for later frontend requests, or support both?
- Do SAML, Celery, fixtures and messaging stay in `rn-forge-django`?
