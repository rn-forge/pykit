# F20.2 — Dependency risk

Find vulnerable and unacceptably licensed dependencies before a release. CI
audits `uv.lock`, Dependabot watches the `uv` ecosystem as well as
`github-actions`, and a licence inventory lists every dependency's licence.

## Design

Decided in [ADR-0015](../../../adr/ADR-0015.md):

- The audit is `pip-audit` over `uv export --frozen`; a known vulnerability
  fails CI.
- The inventory is `pip-licenses`; GPL, AGPL, SSPL and unknown licences fail,
  others are listed.

## Stories

### S20.2.1 — Audit `uv.lock` in CI

**State:** Closed

**Acceptance:** CI fails or reports, as F20.1 decides, on a known-vulnerable
locked dependency; a test or documented run shows a seeded vulnerable pin is
caught.

### S20.2.2 — Dependabot for `uv`

**State:** Closed

**Acceptance:** `.github/dependabot.yml` has a `uv` ecosystem entry beside
`github-actions`, and the file validates.

### S20.2.3 — Licence inventory

**State:** New

**Acceptance:** CI produces a licence inventory of the locked dependencies, and
a licence outside the policy F20.1 sets is flagged.

## Acceptance

- S20.2.1: the audit step runs in CI and behaves as F20.1 decides.
- S20.2.2: `.github/dependabot.yml` lists `uv` and `github-actions`.
- S20.2.3: the inventory artifact is produced on the main CI run.
